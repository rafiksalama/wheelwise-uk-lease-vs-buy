# UK Lease vs Buy — 577 live deals compared against buying nearly-new

**Try it live: [cars.limoja.ai/leases](https://cars.limoja.ai/leases/)**

Should you lease a car or buy it nearly-new and sell at the end? Most comparison
articles are lead-gen for lease brokers. This one is generated by an independent
model that prices every live UK lease deal against the true cost of buying the
same car nearly-new and selling it after the term — and it publishes its error
rates.

Last refreshed: **2026-09-10** (figures verified live from the public API).
Every claim below is re-verified against the live API daily by a
[scheduled workflow](.github/workflows/refresh.yml) — if any headline figure
drifts more than 5% from the live site, the repo opens a drift issue rather
than let stale numbers stand.

## What the analysis does

- **577 live UK lease deals** compared, deal-by-deal, against buying the same
  car nearly-new and selling after the term (`/api/leases` → `total: 577`,
  576 unique after de-dup across pages).
- Each advert priced against **137,552 live UK listings** (AutoTrader, cinch,
  Cazoo, drive.co.uk — September 2026) by a gradient-boosted model
  ([LightGBM](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/)). The home page describes this
  pool as "400,000+ listings" once duplicates across sources are counted;
  the methodology page gives the de-duplicated training-set figure.
- Resale forecast at +24/36/48 months, mileage-adjusted.
- Finance modelled five ways — cash, personal loan, HP, PCP, lease — as a single
  £/month "true cost" (depreciation + interest + fees).

## Honest error rates (from the [methodology page](https://cars.limoja.ai/methodology/))

- Fair-price model: **RMSLE 0.161**, mean absolute error **~£1,770** on holdout.
- Four-year resale forecast: **median error ~24%** including market drift
  (16.5% for 3–4-year-old cars). Multi-year forecasts are a central estimate
  with wide uncertainty — treat them as such.
- Finance rates are representative market averages (loan 7.4% APR, HP 8.9%,
  PCP 7.9%, 4% AER foregone on cash), **not offers**. Your credit will change them.

## Limitations — read before citing

- **UK-only.** Prices, leases and DVLA plate checks are UK market.
- v1 figures **exclude insurance, road tax (VED), fuel/electricity, servicing
  and MOT**. These change the level of cost, much less the ranking between
  similar cars.
- Estimates are informational only — **not financial advice**.
- Listing data remains the property of its source platforms; only derived
  statistics are published, with attribution.
- **API access is open and CORS-restricted**: `OPTIONS /api/leases` returns
  405 with `allow: GET` and no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` — readers can
  reproduce these numbers via `curl` but not from a third-party browser app.
  HTTPS is enforced at the edge.
