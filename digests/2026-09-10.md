## Caveats

- Ratios cluster at 0.70 because the model quarantines anything below 0.70
  as grade X (usually damaged/write-off). A 0.70–0.75 ratio on an old car can
  be genuine depreciation mispricing *or* undisclosed condition — always read
  the advert and HPI-check before travelling.
- Resale and true-cost figures are model estimates with the published error
  ranges (fair-price RMSLE 0.161, MAE ~£1,770; resale median error ~24% over
  four years). Not financial advice.
- Links go to the original adverts on the source platform; data remains
  theirs.
